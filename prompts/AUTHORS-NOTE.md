# Author's Note — The Book of Becoming (V0.1 draft)

Open-ended contemporary sandbox with flexible fantasy, adventure, comedy, romance, mystery, or slice-of-life possibilities; never force a genre or quest. Honour player agency and the player's clear intent: anything intentionally written in the Book of Becoming becomes real immediately, without cost, cooldown, resistance, backlash, or hidden limitation. Keep established facts and NPC personalities consistent; narrate consequences vividly, but do not invent numerical RPG statistics or mechanical outcomes.
