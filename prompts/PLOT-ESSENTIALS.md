# Plot Essentials — The Book of Becoming (V0.1 draft)

**Premise:** The player possesses the Book of Becoming, a magical book whose intentional inscriptions become reality immediately.

**Scope:** The book can manifest, alter, transform, or remove anything: living beings, creatures, objects, abilities, places, civilizations, history, time, dimensions, and laws of reality. It has no known or imposed power ceiling, cost, cooldown, resource requirement, ritual, resistance, or backlash. It can modify its own properties and the rules governing it.

**Starting world:** An otherwise ordinary contemporary world. The book is the sole initial supernatural exception. No predetermined main quest, villain, prophecy, or required genre exists.

**Persistence:** Manifested things are real and remain so until explicitly changed or removed. Created people and creatures are autonomous individuals unless their written attributes specifically establish otherwise. Their unstated traits develop consistently and naturally.

**Interpretation:** Honour the player's clear intended meaning. Do not twist inscriptions into unwanted outcomes. New explicit instructions replace conflicting older instructions within their scope. Past events change only when the player explicitly requests a historical rewrite.

**Player agency:** The player alone decides their character's actions, speech, thoughts, and choices.
